TwoTrios – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 02/10/2026

This policy explains what information the TwoTrios game (“the app”, on Android and iOS) collects, why, and what you can do about it.

TwoTrios is published by Raul Carreira Rufato, who is responsible for the data described here (the “data controller”). Contact: twotriosgame@gmail.com.

1. Your game account

When you first open the app, it creates an anonymous guest account for you, so your stars are kept safe. We store:

  • a random account identifier (it doesn't reveal who you are);
  • your star balance, and records of stars earned (match wins, rewarded ads), spent (jokers, undos) and bought;
  • which phone your account is currently signed in on (a session identifier), so an account is used on one phone at a time.

If you choose to save your stars with an email address, we also store that email address and use it only to send you sign-in codes. We never send marketing emails.

2. Purchases

Star packs are paid through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. We receive the store's purchase or transaction identifier and the product bought, and check them with Google or Apple, so your stars are added once and only once.

3. Playing with friends

When you play with friends, the name you type and the game moves are sent live to the other players in your room. They pass through our game server but are not stored: they disappear when the match ends.

4. Help messages

If you send us a message with the HELP button, we receive the name, email address and message you type, together with your random account identifier and whether you use Android or iOS. The message is saved on our game server and sent to our mailbox, and we use it only to answer you. Sending a help message is always optional.

5. Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: full-screen ads between games, and optional ads you can choose to watch for stars. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

  • In the EU, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested. You can refuse personalised ads; you will then see ads that are not based on your interests.
  • On iPhone, the app asks for permission to track you (Apple's pop-up). If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, your advertising identifier is not shared. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • When you watch an optional ad for stars, Google tells our server, with your random account identifier, that the ad was watched to the end, so we can add the stars.

6. What stays on your phone

The app keeps a few things on your phone only: the last known star count (so the game works offline), game progress counters, and your settings.

7. Who processes the data

  • Supabase hosts our game server and database (supabase.com/privacy).
  • Resend sends the sign-in codes to the email address you give us, and delivers help messages to our mailbox (resend.com/legal/privacy-policy).
  • Google (AdMob, Google Play) and Apple (App Store), as described above.

We do not sell your data. Some of these providers may process data outside your country, with the safeguards required by law (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

8. Why we use it (legal bases)

  • Your account, stars and purchases: to provide the game you asked for (contract).
  • Help messages: our legitimate interest in answering the players who write to us.
  • Personalised ads and tracking: only with your consent, which you can withdraw.
  • Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention (e.g. stars credited only once): our legitimate interest in funding and protecting the game.

9. How long we keep it

We keep your account data as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed. We keep only the anonymous record that a purchase or rewarded ad was already credited, so it can never be credited twice.

Help messages, with the name and email address you typed, are kept for up to 12 months so we can follow up on your request, and are then deleted. They are not removed automatically when you delete your account; write to us if you want them deleted sooner.

10. Your rights and deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app (home screen → your account → Delete account). You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your data, to receive a copy of it, to restrict its use or to object to its use, by writing to twotriosgame@gmail.com. Where we rely on your consent (personalised ads and tracking), you can withdraw it at any time, as explained in the Ads section, or by writing to us. If you are in the EU, you may also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

11. Children

TwoTrios is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
